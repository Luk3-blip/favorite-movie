Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone

Introduction

Harry Potter and the Sorcerer's Stone is one of my favorite movies. It is a fantasy movie about a young wizard named Harry Potter.

Plot

Harry Potter discovers that he is a wizard and begins studying at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He makes new friends and discovers a mystery involving the Sorcerer's Stone.

Cast

Daniel Radcliffe as Harry Potter

Emma Watson as Hermione Granger

Rupert Grint as Ron Weasley

Review

I like this movie because it has magic, adventure, friendship, and an interesting story. It is a fun movie to watch.
